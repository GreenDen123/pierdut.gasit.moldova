# Privacy Policy

**Pierdut @ Găsit Moldova**

Effective date: **1 October 2026**

---

## 1. Who processes your data

The data controller is the independent developer **GreenAppStudio**, author and owner of the Pierdut @ Găsit Moldova app (the "App").

Contact for any data-related question: **suport.pierdut.gasit@gmail.com**

Processing is carried out in accordance with **Law No. 195/2024 of the Republic of Moldova on the protection of personal data**, in force since 23 August 2026, which transposes Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) into national law.

This Policy is available in Romanian, Russian and English; in the event of a discrepancy, the **Romanian** version prevails.

---

## 2. The short version

We collect the minimum data needed to run the App: your account details, the content of your listings and comments, your crystal balance and technical information about your device. We **do not sell** your data.

Some of the data you publish yourself, and it is visible to all users: your account name, the text and photos of your listing, the map pin, the publication date and your comments. Comments in the App are **public**, not private messages. Please do not post anything there you are not prepared to show to strangers — and above all, no personal data of other people taken from found items (section 8).

## 3. What data we process

**3.1. Account**
Name or pseudonym, e-mail address, password (stored only as an irreversible hash by the Supabase authentication service), registration date, interface language. If you sign in with **Google Sign-In**, we receive your Google account identifier, name and e-mail address; your Google password is never shared with us.

**3.2. Listings**
Status ("Lost", "Found", "Recovered", "Returned to owner"), category, title, description, photos, map pin coordinates, locality and landmark, publication date and expiry, whether "URGENT" promotion is active.

**3.3. Comments**
Comment text, author, date. Comments are public.

**3.4. Crystals**
Current balance and transaction history: credits for publishing listings and watching rewarded ads, debits for promotion and extension, cancellations imposed as a sanction.

**3.5. Technical data**
Device model, OS and app version, language, IP address, installation identifier, push notification token, crash reports, anonymised usage events.

**3.6. Advertising identifier**
The Android advertising ID (AAID) — used to serve ads and to count rewarded ads watched.

**3.7. Requests and reports**
E-mail address, the content of your request, attached screenshots, correspondence, as well as the content of reports about other users and the outcome of the review.

**3.8. We do not collect** special categories of data (health, beliefs, biometrics) and we do not process payment data — there are no payments in the App: crystals are granted free of charge and are not sold for money.

## 5. What other users can see

**Public:** your account name, the title, description and photos of your listing, its status and category, the map pin, the publication date, the "URGENT" badge, and your comments.

**Not published:** your e-mail address, password, Google account identifier, crystal balance and history, technical identifiers, your device's precise location, and reports you file about other users.

Tip: use a pseudonym if you prefer not to be recognised, and discuss details in comments rather than publishing your personal phone number.

## 6. Where data is stored and who processes it

We do not sell or share data with third parties for advertising purposes. The following providers process data on our behalf:

- **Supabase** — database, photo storage and authentication. Accounts, listings, comments and crystals are stored here. Servers are located in the **European Union**.
- **Google Firebase** (Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics) — push notification delivery, crash reports and anonymised usage statistics.
- **Google AdMob** — serving ads and rewarded ads.
- **Google Sign-In** — signing in to the App with a Google account.
- **Law enforcement and other public authorities** — only upon a reasoned lawful request.

Google's processing of data is governed by its own privacy policies.

## 7. Advertising and the advertising identifier

7.1. The App is free and is funded by Google AdMob advertising.

7.2. Ads may be personalised, based on your device's advertising identifier. You can limit this at any time in your device settings: **Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID** (or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"). Ads will still appear, but will be less relevant.

7.3. Watching rewarded ads is voluntary. We record only the fact that an ad was watched in full — in order to grant the crystal.

7.4. We do not share your name, e-mail address or listing content with advertisers.

## 8. Third-party data in your listings

8.1. A found item almost always contains another person's personal data. **Publishing it is prohibited:** the owner's name and surname, IDNP, document series and number, bank card number, address, phone number, as well as photos of documents and cards.

8.2. Correct: "found a wallet with documents and cards — describe the contents to confirm it is yours." Incorrect: listing the owner's name and the details from their documents.

8.3. By publishing such information you become personally responsible for processing another person's data. We delete such listings immediately and without warning and apply sanctions to the author.

8.4. If you find your own data published by someone else in the App, write to **suport.pierdut.gasit@gmail.com** — we will remove it within **3 business days**.

|---|---|

| Comments | Deleted together with the listing |

## 10. Your rights

Under Law No. 195/2024 you have the right to: **access** your data and obtain a copy; **rectify** inaccurate data; **erase** it; **restrict** processing; **object** to processing based on legitimate interest; receive your data in a machine-readable format (**portability**); **withdraw consent**; and lodge a **complaint with the supervisory authority**.

**How to exercise them:** most rights can be exercised directly in the App — **Profile → My listings** (edit or delete a post) and **Profile → Delete account**. Send any other request to **suport.pierdut.gasit@gmail.com** from the e-mail address registered to your account (this is needed to verify your identity). We respond within **30 days**; for complex requests the period may be extended with notice.

**Supervisory authority:** National Centre for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Moldova (CNPDCP), **datepersonale.md**.

## 11. Security

Data in transit is protected with HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Database access is restricted by row-level security policies and is available only to the developer. We do not keep data in plain form on personal devices.

No service can guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that creates a risk to your rights, we will notify the CNPDCP within **72 hours** of becoming aware of it and, where the risk is high, we will notify you as well.

## 12. Age restriction

The App is intended for persons **over 18** and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you become aware that a minor has registered in the App, please tell us at **suport.pierdut.gasit@gmail.com** — the account and related data will be deleted.
